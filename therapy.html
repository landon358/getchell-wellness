You don't need to be in crisis to start.

Most of the men I work with aren't. They're functioning. They show up. And they're exhausted from holding it together.

  • You lost someone, and everyone around you has moved on.
  • You lead a crew and there's nobody you can be honest with about how heavy that is.
  • You're in your twenties and you feel more isolated than anyone knows.
  • Your partner or your mom told you to talk to someone, and you're finally willing.
  • You heard a Hope in Hard Hats talk and recognized yourself in it.

No couch. No jargon. No "and how does that make you feel."

You'll get someone trained in the clinic who talks like someone from the job site, because I am.

Structured, not soft.

Trained in CBT, DBT, and trauma-informed care at Michigan Medicine. We set goals, we track them, and you leave each session with something to do.
