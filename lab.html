Austin's story

I lost my brother Austin at sixteen.

He was the youngest of us. Ginger hair, a big laugh, a tight circle of friends. He died by suicide in March 2022, a month after his birthday.

That loss could have ended me. Instead it pulled me out of the trades and into social work and psychiatry, so I could understand what happened and make sure another family doesn't have to go through it. Everything I bring to a job site comes from that. I'm not teaching theory. I'm sharing what I've lived.
